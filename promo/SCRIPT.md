# Big Timber TikTok — voiceover script

Record on your phone (Voice Memos is fine) in a quiet room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
One take is fine: read each line, then **pause about one second** between lines. Casual, like you're telling a friend.
Flubbed a line? Just pause and say it again; the splitter keeps the last clean take if you tell me which.

1. I went to the Trees of Mystery and stood under a forty-nine foot Paul Bunyan.

3. So I quickly cooked up a game to scratch the itch.
4. You chop trees as Paul.
5. Hit the glowing knots for crits, and keep the rhythm for combos.
6. Then you ride Babe the Blue Ox to the sawmill.
7. Drift the corners, jump the logs, and don't spill your cargo.
8. Sell the lumber, build the camp, and hire the whole crew.
9. Yes. Including Sport, the Reversible Dog.
10. It's free in your browser. Link's in the comments.
11. Go fell some trees.
